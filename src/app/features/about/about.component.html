Our Story

A New Destination for Relaxation

Welcome to Green Field Resort Hotel, a premier inland resort nestled in the serene municipality of Anda, Bohol. Founded in 2026 by Rosamaria R. Bramhall, the resort was established with a vision of creating a peaceful sanctuary where nature, comfort, and genuine Filipino hospitality come together.

Surrounded by lush tropical landscapes and a tranquil atmosphere, Green Field Resort Hotel offers an ideal retreat for couples, families, friends, and travelers seeking relaxation away from the crowds. Whether you're planning a weekend getaway, a family vacation, or a special celebration, our thoughtfully designed accommodations, inviting swimming pools, exceptional dining, and personalized service ensure a memorable experience for every guest.

At Green Field Resort Hotel, we believe that true luxury lies in comfort, heartfelt hospitality, and meaningful moments. Every detail of our resort reflects our commitment to providing a welcoming environment where guests can unwind, reconnect with nature, and create lasting memories.

We invite you to discover the charm of inland living, experience the warmth of our hospitality, and make Green Field Resort Hotel your home away from home in the heart of Bohol.
