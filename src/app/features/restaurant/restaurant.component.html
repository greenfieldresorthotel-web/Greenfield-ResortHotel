Our Philosophy

Fresh Flavors, Crafted with Care

At Green Field Restaurant, we believe that exceptional dining begins with fresh ingredients, skilled preparation, and genuine Filipino hospitality. Our culinary team is dedicated to creating flavorful dishes & beverages that celebrate both local traditions and international favorites, ensuring every meal is as memorable as your stay.

Inspired by the rich culture and natural beauty of Bohol, we thoughtfully prepare each dish using carefully selected ingredients to deliver quality, freshness, and authentic taste. From hearty Filipino specialties to international classics, every plate is crafted with attention to detail and a passion for excellent food.

Whether you're enjoying a leisurely breakfast, a relaxing lunch by the pool, or a delightful dinner with family and friends, Green Field Restaurant offers a welcoming dining experience where great food and warm hospitality come together to create lasting memories.
